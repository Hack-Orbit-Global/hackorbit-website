# 01 — Product Vision

## Vision
A global, credible, online-first organisation where developers build real things, contribute to shared open-source work, and carry a permanent, verifiable record of what they've done — starting small, structured to scale.

## Mission
Give builders a legitimate home for public, contribution-based growth: ship projects, contribute to org repositories, earn recognised badges and certificates, and be discoverable through a public profile that reflects real work, not vanity metrics.

## Problem
- Individual open-source contribution is scattered across many repos with no unifying, portable, verifiable record
- Most "developer community" sites are either pure content (blogs/newsletters) with no real participation loop, or heavyweight platforms that over-promise identity/social features they can't operate at small scale
- Certificates and achievement claims across hackathon/community spaces are frequently unverifiable or easily faked
- New builders lack a low-friction way to demonstrate a track record of real, org-recognised contribution

## Solution
Hack Orbit provides:
- A single verified identity per person (Google-anchored, GitHub + Discord connected) tied to a permanent public Member ID
- A public, shareable profile showing real contributions, official vs. community project involvement, badges, and certificates
- Org-repo-centric GitHub contribution tracking that's accurate and scalable without invasive full-account scanning
- Publicly verifiable certificates (metadata-only verification, not downloadable-file spoofing)
- A clear, honest distinction between official Hack Orbit work and independent community work

## Target Users
- **Early-career developers / students** who want a real, verifiable contribution record and mentorship-adjacent community
- **Open-source contributors** looking for an organised, well-scoped place to contribute (vs. hunting random repos)
- **Project builders** who want their work showcased and potentially adopted as an official Hack Orbit project
- **Community organisers / future collaborators** evaluating Hack Orbit's legitimacy (via founder credit, SEO presence, verifiable certificates)
- **The founder (Sabarna Barik)**, who needs the system to be operable solo without enterprise overhead

## Organisation Positioning
Hack Orbit positions itself as an **open-source technology organisation and developer community** — not a company, not an ed-tech product, not an events business (yet). It is community- and contribution-first, professionally presented, and globally oriented from day one even while operationally small.

Hack Orbit is explicitly **technology-neutral**: web development, DevOps, AI/ML, and technology work in general are all equally part of the organisation's scope. The "Hack" in the name should read as "build/create," not as a cybersecurity or hacking specialism — no page, project category, or visual direction should imply otherwise.

## Value Proposition
- For builders: a durable, portable, verifiable record of real contribution — one link (`/profile/HO-000001`) that means something
- For the organisation: a credible, SEO-legible public identity that scales without requiring enterprise infrastructure before it's earned
- For the founder: clear, natural, non-spammy founder credit tied to a legitimate organisational history

## Long-Term Direction
Start as a single online organisation website with identity, profiles, and contribution tracking. Layer in larger hackathons, AI summits, and offline events later as **separate, independently-built sites**, linked from — but not entangled with — the main site's architecture. Migrate off Google Sheets to a proper database once member/contribution volume requires it, without disrupting the public-facing identity model.

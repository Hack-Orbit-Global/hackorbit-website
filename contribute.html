Contributing Guide

Our automated systems trace, review, and reward open-source contributions directly via our digital ledger.

01
Authentication

Establish Member Signature

Begin by completing our secure OAuth registration flow. Connecting your Google, GitHub, and Discord identities links your public commits to your profile automatically.

Register ID
02
Coordination

Claim or Propose Issues

Explore active repositories inside the projects portal. You can pick unresolved issues labeled with contribution difficulty or submit RFC whitepapers on Discord to build new modules.

03
Validation

Commit & Capture

Push your commits and open a Pull Request. Once successfully approved and merged, our webhooks synchronize the state with the Apps Script ledger, awarding badges or certificates.

Projects

Official & community projects

Hack Orbit hosts two kinds of projects, kept deliberately distinct: official projects owned and maintained by the organisation, and community projects led by members. Contributions to official repositories are always welcome.

Official

Official Hack Orbit projects

Owned and maintained by Hack Orbit in our GitHub organisation. Official projects are where the organisation's core work happens — merged pull requests, resolved issues, and reviews all count as contributions.

Community

Community projects

Proposed and led by Hack Orbit members. Community projects are showcased with a clear label so the distinction from official work is never ambiguous. A community project can be adopted as official over time.

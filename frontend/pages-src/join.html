Member verification

Join Hack Orbit

Becoming a verified member takes three quick steps. Each connection proves ownership of an account you already use — and together they guarantee one real person, one Member ID, one public profile.

Verification steps

Connect Google

Google is the anchor of your identity. We only request your basic profile — never access to your email contents.

Connect GitHub

Links your GitHub handle so future contributions to Hack Orbit's repositories can be attributed to your Member ID.

Connect Discord

Confirms your place in the Hack Orbit community. Your Discord ID is stored internally and never shown publicly.

Start by connecting your Google account to begin verification.

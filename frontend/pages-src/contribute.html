How to contribute to Hack Orbit

Contribution is at the heart of Hack Orbit. Here's exactly how to go from visitor to recognised contributor — with your work reflected on a public, verifiable profile.

Five steps to your first contribution

Verify your identity

Complete the three-step verification flow (Google → GitHub → Discord) to receive your permanent Member ID. This is what ties your future contributions to your profile.

Find a project

Browse the Projects page for official Hack Orbit repositories. Official repos are where your contributions are tracked automatically.

Pick a good first issue

Look for issues labelled good first issue or help wanted in the official repositories. Introduce yourself, ask questions, and claim an issue before starting.

Open a pull request

Fork the repository, make your change, and open a pull request. Follow each repo's contributing guide and keep PRs focused and well-tested.

Get recognised

When your pull request merges, the contribution is attributed to your Member ID automatically — earning you badges and building your public contribution record.

Contribution guidelines

Do

  • · Discuss significant changes before implementing them
  • · Write tests alongside your changes
  • · Keep pull requests small and focused
  • · Be respectful and constructive in reviews
  • · Ask for help when you're stuck — that's what a community is for

Avoid

  • · Opening PRs without reading the contributing guide
  • · Scope-creeping a PR beyond its stated purpose
  • · Claiming issues you haven't started
  • · Submitting the same contribution to multiple organisations
What gets tracked

Contributions that count on your profile

Hack Orbit tracks contribution activity within its official GitHub organisation's repositories. Merged pull requests, closed issues you resolved, and submitted code reviews are all attributed to your Member ID and shown on your public profile.

We deliberately do not scan members' personal GitHub accounts. Recognition is based on work done within Hack Orbit's official repositories — accurate to what matters, and scalable by design.

About Hack Orbit

An open-source organisation for builders.

Hack Orbit is an open-source technology organisation and developer community. It was founded on a simple idea: that people who build things should have a real, participatory home — a place to ship projects, contribute to shared open-source work, and grow together as a community.

Our philosophy

Build. Contribute. Orbit together.

Build

We believe in shipping real things. Members build projects that range across the technology spectrum — web development, DevOps, AI/ML, and general software engineering.

Contribute

We believe in shared work. Contributions to Hack Orbit's official repositories are tracked, recognised, and reflected on every member's public profile.

Orbit together

We believe in community. Growth happens collectively — through collaboration, mentoring, and building a durable record of work that members can carry forward.

Positioning

Technology-neutral by design.

Hack Orbit is an engineering organisation, not a cybersecurity brand. The word "hack" is used in its original sense — to build, create, and tinker — not to imply security or breaching. Our community spans web development, DevOps, AI/ML, and technology broadly, and no single domain is privileged over the others.

Hack Orbit is not a cybersecurity or hacking-focused organisation. It is not an event-management platform, and it is not a certificate-download portal. It is a community where developers build and are recognised for real, verifiable work.

History

From a founding idea to an open community.

Hack Orbit was founded by Sabarna Barik as a from-scratch initiative with a clear mission: give builders a participatory home and a durable, verifiable record of their open-source work. The organisation is built around a verified identity system — one real person, one Member ID, one public profile — anchored by Google and confirmed through GitHub and Discord.

Today Hack Orbit is an online-first organisation, operated by its founder and built to scale into a global developer community — while remaining honest about what it is and never fabricating its numbers.

Sabarna Barik

Sabarna Barik is the founder of Hack Orbit. The organisation grew out of a belief that open source is one of the most reliable ways to build systems that are resilient, transparent, and long-lived — and that the people who contribute to them deserve a credible, verifiable record of that work.

Under Sabarna's leadership, Hack Orbit is built around three commitments: precision (engineering without compromise), performance (fast, accessible, high-quality software), and purpose (building things that matter to a community).
